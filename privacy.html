Privacy & data

Your nutrition data belongs to you.

Nourish is designed for personal use and keeps the core of your tracking history on your own device.

Last updated: 9 August 2026

On this page What is stored AI Coach Your controls Health guidance
01

What is stored on your device

Your profile, nutrition targets, meal logs, water logs, custom foods, preferences and recent Coach messages are stored in your browser’s local storage. Nourish does not require an account for its core tracking features.

Meal photos chosen through the camera are analyzed inside your browser and are not uploaded by Nourish. The first scan downloads the free TensorFlow.js and MobileNet recognition software. Nourish keeps only the food you confirm—not the meal photo.

Clearing browser or website data may remove this information. Use the export feature in Settings to create a backup you control.

02

How the AI Coach uses information

When live AI is enabled and you send a message, Nourish sends the message plus a limited summary of your current nutrition totals, remaining targets, dietary preference and declared allergens to the configured AI service. It does not need your name, precise body measurements or complete meal history to answer.

The Coach’s live connection is optional. Tracking, insights and rule-based suggestions continue to work without it.

03

You stay in control

  • Export a readable backup at any time.
  • Restore a backup on another device.
  • Delete individual entries or erase all app data.
  • Use the app offline without sending Coach requests.
04

Wellness guidance, not medical advice

Nourish provides general educational estimates and habit support. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent disease and cannot replace advice from a qualified clinician or registered dietitian.

Food composition varies by brand, preparation and portion. Confirm values from packaging or a trusted food database when precision matters. Seek professional guidance for allergies, pregnancy, medical conditions, medication interactions or eating-disorder concerns.
